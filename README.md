# Teradyne Equity Research

A sell-side style research report on Teradyne (NASDAQ: TER), written for the Asset Management Practicum (MBA AF 632) at UMass Boston in Spring 2025 using Bloomberg Terminal data. The report rates the stock a Buy with an $89.79 target price, about 21% above the $74.21 share price on May 5, 2025. In less than six months the stock had more than doubled.

| | |
|---|---|
| Rating | Buy |
| Share price (May 5, 2025) | $74.21 |
| Target price | $89.79 (about 21% upside) |
| Valuation method | EV/EBITDA, cross-checked against peer multiples |
| Sector | Semiconductor equipment |

## The thesis

Teradyne makes the automated test equipment that every advanced chip has to pass before it ships. As AI accelerators, EV power modules, and datacenter processors get more complex and run hotter, testing gets harder and costlier to skip. The report calls this the "AHA moment": no test, no tech. Teradyne is a picks-and-shovels play on AI, EVs, and automation rather than a bet on any single end market.

Roughly 70% of revenue comes from semiconductor test. Nearly 20% comes from industrial automation through Universal Robots and Mobile Industrial Robots, which gives the company a second growth leg and a track record of buying into automation early.

## Valuation

The target applies an 18x EV/EBITDA multiple to 2025E EBITDA of $950M.

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| Enterprise value | $17.1B |
| Equity value (including about $1.4B net cash) | $18.5B |
| Implied share price | $89.79 |

Peer multiples used to frame the 18x:

| Company | EV/EBITDA | P/E | EV/Sales |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teradyne | 18.0x | 24.5x | 5.1x |
| Advantest | 39.7x | 36.2x | 9.4x |
| Applied Materials | 13.8x | 19.8x | 3.8x |
| Keysight | 16.5x | 21.0x | 4.3x |

Sensitivity to the multiple:

| EV/EBITDA | 15x | 16x | 17x | 18x | 19x | 20x | 21x |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implied price | $76.34 | $80.98 | $85.61 | $89.79 | $94.88 | $99.51 | $104.15 |

Even at 15x the implied price sits above the $74.21 entry price, which is why the risk and reward looked favorable.

## Other analysis in the report

- **DuPont decomposition**\
  Breaks return on equity into tax burden, interest burden, operating margin, asset turnover, and leverage.
- **Quarterly revenue and EPS trend**\
  Covers 2021 to 2024, with year-over-year growth for each quarter.
- **Porter's Five Forces**\
  Applied to the semiconductor test and industrial robotics markets.
- **Risk factors**\
  Cyclical demand, customer concentration (top five customers were 36% of 2024 revenue), competition, FX, export controls tied to China, and M&A integration.
- **ESG profile**\
  Bloomberg scores, with an overall score of 4.83 and governance the strongest pillar at 7.81.
